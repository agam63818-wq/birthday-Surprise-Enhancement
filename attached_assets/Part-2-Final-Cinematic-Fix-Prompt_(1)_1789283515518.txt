7.  Keep the rose centered optically, not just mathematically.
8.  Ensure stem terminates naturally into the flower head.
9.  Ensure leaves attach to the stem.
10. Ensure petals bloom around a consistent flower center.
11. Ensure the flower does not collide with the text.
