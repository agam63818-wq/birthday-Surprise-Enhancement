AUDIO FILES — INSTRUCTIONS
==========================

Place your audio files here:

  background-music.mp3  →  Plays quietly in the background throughout the experience
  birthday-song.mp3     →  Plays when the birthday cake is tapped

If no audio files are provided, the app will use built-in synthesized tones as a fallback.

TIPS:
- Use soft piano / lo-fi music for background-music.mp3
- Use a short happy birthday tune for birthday-song.mp3
- Keep files under 5MB for fast loading on mobile
