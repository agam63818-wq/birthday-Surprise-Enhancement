IMAGES — INSTRUCTIONS
======================

Place custom images here (teddy, cake, decorations, etc.).

To use a custom cake image:
  1. Place your image here as "cake.png" or "cake.jpg"
  2. Update PageCake.tsx to use <img src="/images/cake.png" ... />

To use custom teddy/character images:
  1. Place them here e.g. "teddy1.png", "teddy2.png"
  2. Update MochiCat.tsx to use <img src="/images/teddy1.png" ... />
